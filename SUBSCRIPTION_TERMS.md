# BiteBuddy Subscription Terms

Version: subscription_terms_v1 · Effective: July 5, 2026

## Plans

BiteBuddy offers an optional auto-renewing subscription, **BiteBuddy Pro**, in monthly and annual options. Current pricing is always shown in the app before purchase. The free tier currently includes 10 AI scans per week; Pro currently includes 100 AI scans per day plus the features listed on the paywall. Feature availability and limits may change over time; material reductions will be communicated in the app.

## Billing through Apple

All purchases are processed by Apple through your Apple ID. Payment is charged when you confirm the purchase. We never receive your full payment details.

## Auto-renewal and cancellation

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel any time in your device's Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep Pro access until the current paid period ends. Deleting the app or your BiteBuddy account does **not** cancel an Apple subscription.

## Free trials

No free trial is offered at launch. If a free trial is introduced later, its length and the price after the trial will be shown before you subscribe, and unused trial time is forfeited when you purchase, per Apple's rules.

## Refunds

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple under Apple's terms. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. Where law requires us to provide refunds directly, we will comply.

## Restore purchases

Use "Restore Purchases" on the paywall to re-activate Pro on a new device or after reinstalling, signed in with the same Apple ID.

## What Pro is not

Pro increases limits and convenience. It does **not** make nutrition estimates medically reliable, guaranteed accurate, or suitable for health-critical decisions. All disclaimers in the Terms of Service and Medical Disclaimer apply equally to paid features.
