# AI Nutrition Estimate Consent

Version: ai_estimate_consent_v1 · Effective: July 5, 2026

Before you use BiteBuddy's scanning features, we want you to understand exactly how they work.

## AI is doing the estimating

BiteBuddy uses artificial intelligence (Google's Gemini models) together with public nutrition databases (USDA FoodData Central, Open Food Facts) to estimate the foods, portions, and nutrition in what you scan. No human expert reviews your scans.

## What is processed

When you scan, the following is sent securely to our server and, as needed, to the AI provider:

- the food or label photo you take or import,
- any barcode detected,
- any text or voice-transcribed description and corrections you add,
- your nutrition targets and goal context (to tailor insights), and
- the resulting estimate, which is saved only if you log it.

Photos are used to produce the estimate and are not retained as images afterward. Your data is not used to train AI models.

## Estimates can be wrong

AI estimates are approximations. They can misidentify foods, miss ingredients (especially oils, dressings, and sauces), and misjudge portions. Every result screen lets you review, edit, and correct the estimate — please do this before saving, and treat totals as informational estimates rather than measurements.

## Not for health-critical decisions

BiteBuddy does not provide medical, dietitian, allergy, or emergency advice. Do not rely on scan results for allergy safety, diabetes dosing, eating-disorder recovery, sodium-restricted or therapeutic diets, pregnancy nutrition, or any other health-critical decision. Consult a qualified professional for those decisions.

## Your consent

By tapping accept, you consent to this AI processing of your scans and confirm you understand that estimates may be inaccurate and that BiteBuddy is not medical advice. You can stop using scanning at any time; manual entry never requires AI.
