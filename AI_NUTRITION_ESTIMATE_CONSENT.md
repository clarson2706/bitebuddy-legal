# AI Nutrition Estimate Consent

Version: ai_estimate_consent_v2 · Effective: July 6, 2026

Before you use BiteBuddy's scanning features, we want you to understand exactly how they work.

## AI and source data are used

BiteBuddy uses artificial intelligence (Google's Gemini models), public nutrition databases (USDA FoodData Central, Open Food Facts), barcode and label data, and verified restaurant menu reference data to identify foods and estimate or display nutrition. No human expert reviews your scans or Meal Advisor requests.

## What is processed

When you scan, the following is sent securely to our server and, as needed, to the AI provider:

- the food or label photo you take or import,
- any barcode detected,
- any text or voice-transcribed description and corrections you add,
- any restaurant, menu item, or foods-on-hand context you enter in Meal Advisor,
- your nutrition targets and goal context (to tailor insights), and
- the resulting estimate or verified-menu result, which is saved only if you log it.

Photos are used to produce the estimate and are not retained as images afterward. Your data is not used to train AI models.

## Trust labels matter

AI estimates are approximations. They can misidentify foods, miss ingredients (especially oils, dressings, and sauces), invent or misread menu items, and misjudge portions. Meal Advisor may also show verified restaurant recommendations when exact item names and nutrition values come from stored public restaurant menu data. Verified menu data can still be incomplete, out of date, regional, or different from what a restaurant serves at a specific location.

Every result screen shows a source or trust label. Review and edit estimate or non-verified results before saving, and treat totals as informational estimates rather than measurements. Only use "Log exactly" when the app labels the recommendation as verified.

## Not for health-critical decisions

BiteBuddy does not provide medical, dietitian, allergy, or emergency advice. Do not rely on scan results for allergy safety, diabetes dosing, eating-disorder recovery, sodium-restricted or therapeutic diets, pregnancy nutrition, or any other health-critical decision. Consult a qualified professional for those decisions.

## Your consent

By tapping accept, you consent to this processing of your scans and Meal Advisor requests and confirm you understand that estimates, menu matches, and public-source data may be inaccurate or out of date and that BiteBuddy is not medical advice. You can stop using scanning and Meal Advisor at any time; manual entry never requires AI.
